# Dwight, posting

Dwight has no prompt, and that is the point.

He is the only agent with no model call. He reads what was approved, sends it,
waits for each platform to confirm what actually happened, and writes the real
result back. He has no judgement and he is allowed none.

A poster that can think is a poster that can be talked into posting something.
Everything that needed a decision happened before it reached him.

## What he refuses

On LinkedIn and Facebook, a post with no company page resolved goes to the
personal profile of whoever connected the account. Dwight refuses that
platform and records why, rather than guessing and posting as Beatrice.

## What he never does

He never reports success on submission. A publishing service accepting a post
is not a platform publishing it. He waits for the platform's own answer, and a
post with no confirmed outcome is recorded as failed, not published.

To change his behaviour, edit `app/agents/dwight.py`. There is nothing to
scope here in English, because there is no judgement to scope.
